# Activation Functions in Neural Networks

This project demonstrates the role of **activation functions** in neural networks and explains how they introduce non-linearity into models, enabling them to learn complex patterns.

---

## Objective
To understand different activation functions and analyze their behavior during neural network training.

---

## What This Notebook Covers
- Why activation functions are required
- Linear vs Non-linear transformations
- Common activation functions:
  - Sigmoid
  - Tanh
  - ReLU
  - Leaky ReLU
  - Softmax
- Visualization of activation functions
- Comparison of activation functions in model training

---

## Key Learnings
- Activation functions introduce non-linearity into neural networks
- ReLU helps mitigate the vanishing gradient problem
- Sigmoid and Tanh are useful but suffer from saturation
- Softmax is commonly used for multi-class classification
